Nanu Local AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Nanu Local AI is designed for private, on-device AI. The app does not require an account, cloud AI service, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or remote inference server.

AI processing

Prompts and generated responses are processed on the Android device by a locally loaded GGUF language model. The app does not upload prompts, model files, or generated responses to a Nanu server.

Model files

When you choose a compatible GGUF model, the app may copy it into the app's private storage so it can be loaded efficiently. Stored models can be removed using the app's model manager, or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

Network access

The current local-inference build does not request the Android INTERNET permission. AI inference therefore does not depend on a network connection.

Content reporting

Assistant responses include a Report action. If you choose it, Nanu Local AI opens your device's email app with the selected response placed into a draft addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you review and send that message yourself. Your email provider's own privacy terms apply to any message you choose to send.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Nanu Local AI does not include analytics, advertising, behavioural tracking, or third-party attribution SDKs in the current release.

Deletion

You can remove stored model files from the app and can erase all app-private data at any time by clearing Nanu Local AI's storage or uninstalling the app.

Generative AI

Outputs are generated by the model selected by the user and can be inaccurate. Users should verify important information, especially professional, medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical information, against authoritative sources.

Contact

Privacy questions and content reports can be sent to nanuai.1991@gmail.com.
