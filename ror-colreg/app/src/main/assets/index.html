Rules 1–41

Lights & day shapes

Power-driven underway

● masthead   ● sidelights   ● sternlight

Masthead light or lights, red and green sidelights, and a white sternlight.

Fishing

GREEN over WHITE = trawling

RED over WHITE = fishing other than trawling

NUC / RAM / constrained by draught

RED over RED = not under command; day shape: two balls.

RED–WHITE–RED = restricted in ability to manoeuvre; day shape: ball-diamond-ball.

Constrained by draught may show three red lights; day shape: cylinder.

Anchor / aground / pilot

Anchor: all-round white light or lights, one ball by day.

Aground: anchor signals plus red over red and three balls.

Pilot: WHITE over RED — “white cap, red nose”.

Sound signals

Manoeuvring

• One short: altering starboard

•• Two short: altering port

••• Three short: operating astern propulsion

••••• Five short: doubt or danger

Restricted visibility

Making way: one prolonged blast at intervals not over two minutes

Underway but stopped: two prolonged blasts at intervals not over two minutes

NUC, RAM, sailing, fishing or towing: one prolonged plus two short

IALA buoyage

Region A

Entering from seaward: red to port, green to starboard.

Region B

Entering from seaward: red to starboard, green to port — “red right returning”.

Cardinal marks

North: cones up/up; East: apart; South: down/down; West: points together.

Quick flashes: North continuous, East 3, South 6 plus long, West 9.

Other marks

Isolated danger: black-red-black, two black balls, white group flashing 2.

Safe water: red-white vertical stripes, red sphere.

Special mark: yellow, X topmark.
